Case closed

Miss Clara Ravenscroft stole The Silver Heron

  • The library window was forced from the inside, so the thief was someone in the house who wanted it to look like a break-in.
  • The small footprints, about a size 5, run from the library window to the conservatory. They can't be Tom Fletcher's size 11 boots, and Mr Hargreaves and Dr Vane were at the card table from 9:00 to 10:15.
  • Dr Vane saw someone in a pale dress cross the garden at about half past nine, and a scrap of lavender silk was caught on the rose in the conservatory. Miss Clara wore lavender silk to dinner, and said she never went out.
  • She had a motive too. She tried to borrow money from the butler last week, then denied having any money trouble.

The painting is back in the library, and Lady Ravenscroft has some hard questions for her niece.
