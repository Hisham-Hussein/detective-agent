Place

The drawing room

The fire has burned low. On the card table lies the score sheet from the evening's game of two-handed whist between Mr Hargreaves and Dr Vane.

The scores are in two different hands, one for each player, with the time written beside each game: from 9:00 to 10:15, never more than ten minutes apart. Neither player could have left the table long enough to cut a painting from its frame, carry it to the conservatory and come back.

The window looks out over the garden towards the conservatory.
