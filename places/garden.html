Place

The garden

The rain eased off at about nine, and the flower beds are soft mud.

Beneath the library window, a line of small footprints, about a size 5, leads away across the lawn to the conservatory. They are light, quick steps, made by narrow shoes with a small heel.

There are large boot prints too, about a size 11. But they only go back and forth along the path between the kitchen garden and the gardener's shed. They never come near the house.
