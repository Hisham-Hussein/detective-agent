Scene of the crime

The library

The empty frame still hangs above the fireplace. The canvas was cut out neatly with a sharp knife, by someone who took their time.

The window is wide open, and the rain has soaked the carpet beneath it. At first sight, a thief climbed in from the garden. But the window's brass latch has been snapped, and the broken pieces lie on the gravel outside. A latch forced from the garden would have fallen into the room.

The window was forced from the inside. Someone in the house broke it open to make the theft look like an outside job.
