Place

The conservatory

It is warm and damp, and full of orange trees in tubs. Small muddy footprints cross the tiled floor to the largest tree and back.

Behind that tree, out of sight from the door, is a long bundle wrapped in a white tablecloth from the dining room. Inside is "The Silver Heron", rolled up and unharmed.

On the thorns of the climbing rose beside the door, a small scrap of lavender silk flutters, torn from a dress or a sleeve.
