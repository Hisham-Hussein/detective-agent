Interview

Mr Hargreaves, the butler

He stands very straight and answers carefully.

I served dinner and cleared the table at nine. Then Dr Vane asked for a game of whist, and her Ladyship had told me to keep the guests amused. We played in the drawing room from nine until a quarter past ten. I never left the table, Detective, and nor did he. The score sheet is still there.

It was I who found the empty frame, at twenty past ten.

I don't like to speak of the family. But since you ask about money: Miss Clara came to me last week and asked whether I could lend her fifty pounds. She said she had debts in London and begged me not to tell her aunt. I said no.
