Interview

Dr Ellis Vane, art dealer

He seems more amused than worried.

Yes, I wanted the Heron. I offered Lady Ravenscroft a very good price last month, and she refused. I'm not ashamed of it. But if I meant to steal a painting, I'd hardly do it at a party where I'm the obvious suspect.

I played whist with Hargreaves in the drawing room from nine until a quarter past ten. He's a shocking player, but an honest one.

One thing, Detective. At about half past nine I glanced out of the drawing room window. Someone in a pale dress was hurrying across the garden towards the conservatory. It was too dark to see a face.
