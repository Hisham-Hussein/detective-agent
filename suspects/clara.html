Interview

Miss Clara Ravenscroft, the niece

She is still wearing the lavender silk dress she wore to dinner, and she is in no mood for questions.

I went up to my room straight after dinner and read until I heard the commotion. I never went out. Why would I, in that weather?

Money? What a vulgar question. My aunt is very generous. I've never needed to borrow a penny from anyone.

If you want a thief, Detective, look at the gardener. He's always lurking about.
