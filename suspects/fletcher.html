Interview

Tom Fletcher, the gardener

He is caked in mud to the knees and looks worried.

I was in my shed all evening, mending the mower and keeping out of the rain. On my own, yes. Nobody can say they saw me.

But I never set foot in the house, Detective. Her Ladyship won't have my boots on her carpets, and I don't blame her. Look at them: size eleven. Every print they've made tonight is on the path between the kitchen garden and my shed.
