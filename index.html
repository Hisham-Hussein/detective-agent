Case file: Ravenscroft Manor

The Silver Heron

On a stormy night, Lady Ravenscroft held a small dinner party. At nine o'clock she went up to bed with a cold, passing her treasured painting, "The Silver Heron", in the library. At twenty past ten the butler found its frame empty, and the library window open to the rain.

Lady Ravenscroft is not a suspect. Four people could have taken the painting. Interview them, inspect the house and grounds, then name the thief. You need the case name in brackets to interview a suspect or inspect a place.

The suspects

  • Mr Hargreaves, the butler (hargreaves)
    He runs the house and was near the library all evening.
  • Dr Ellis Vane, an art dealer (vane)
    He offered to buy the painting last month and was turned down.
  • Tom Fletcher, the gardener (fletcher)
    He came in from the storm covered in mud. Nobody saw him all evening.
  • Miss Clara Ravenscroft, the niece (clara)
    Lady Ravenscroft's devoted niece.

The places

  • The library (library)
    Where the painting hung.
  • The garden (garden)
    Between the house and the conservatory.
  • The conservatory (conservatory)
    The glasshouse at the end of the garden.
  • The drawing room (drawing-room)
    Where the evening's card game was played.
